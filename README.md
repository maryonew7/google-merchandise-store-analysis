# Google Merchandise Store Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of the Google Merchandise Store's sales performance (Feb 16, 2024 – 
Apr 15, 2025) using data from the Google Analytics Demo Account (GA4).

## Contents
- Introduction & Sales Objectives
- Data Exploration (traffic sources, conversion rates, product categories)
- Key Sales Performance Indicators (Conversion Rate, AOV)
- Segmentation Analysis (country, age, gender)
- Analytical Techniques (Descriptive, Comparative, Segmentation, Trend, Correlation)
- Findings, Conclusion & Action Plan

## Key Findings
- **Direct traffic** generates the highest revenue ($1,376,546.29) and best AOV ($145.36)
- **Email** has the highest conversion rate (0.04), followed by Referral (0.03)
- The **U.S.** dominates revenue (91.5%), followed by Canada (4.9%)
- **18–24 age group** shows the highest engagement (12,312 sessions)
- Revenue peaks **Sep–Dec** (back-to-school/holiday season); lowest in Jan–Feb

## Recommendations
- Strengthen direct traffic loyalty & SEO efforts
- Expand referral partnerships
- Focus on organic shopping & email marketing
- Optimize campaigns for high-performing demographics (18–34, U.S. market)
